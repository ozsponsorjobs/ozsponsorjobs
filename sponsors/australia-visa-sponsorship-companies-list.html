🛡️ How to Verify an Australian Sponsor & Protect Against Scams

Before accepting an offer or sending personal identity documents to an overseas recruiter, follow these four statutory verification steps mandated by the Fair Work Ombudsman and Department of Home Affairs:

1. Official ABN Search

Search the company's 11-digit Australian Business Number on the government register (abr.business.gov.au). Ensure the entity status is "Active" and registered for GST.

2. Check SBS Approval

Ask the employer for their Standard Business Sponsorship (SBS) approval notice. Genuine Australian sponsors will provide their Transaction Reference Number (TRN) without hesitation.

3. Zero Recruitment Fees (Strict Law)

Under Section 245AS of the Migration Act 1958, it is a criminal offense in Australia for employers to demand payment for visa sponsorship. If anyone asks you for a "sponsorship fee", report them immediately.

4. Statutory Salary Floor (TSMIT)

Legitimate contracts must state a base salary equal to or greater than AUD $73,150 per year, plus mandatory 11.5% employer Superannuation contributions paid directly into your Australian super fund.
