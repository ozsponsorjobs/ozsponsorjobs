Australian Visa Pathways

  • Subclass 482 (TSS)

    Temporary Skill Shortage visa. Enables approved Australian businesses to sponsor skilled workers for 2 to 4 years with pathway to PR.

  • Subclass 186 (ENS)

    Employer Nomination Scheme. Direct Australian Permanent Residency for highly skilled workers nominated by their employer.

  • Subclass 494 (SESR)

    Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional. 5-year provisional visa in designated regional areas leading directly to Subclass 191 PR.
