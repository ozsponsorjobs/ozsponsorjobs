Australian Visa Programs We Cover

1. Subclass 482 - Skills in Demand / Temporary Skill Shortage (TSS)

The Subclass 482 visa permits approved employers to sponsor an overseas worker to fill positions when an appropriately skilled Australian worker cannot be sourced. This visa provides pathways for up to 2 or 4 years depending on the occupation stream (Core Skills, Specialist Skills, or Essential Skills), with guaranteed transitions toward permanent residency.

2. Subclass 186 - Employer Nomination Scheme (ENS)

The Subclass 186 visa grants direct Australian Permanent Residency (PR). Australian employers can nominate highly experienced candidates through either the Direct Entry (DE) stream or the Temporary Residence Transition (TRT) stream, granting full Medicare access, family relocation privileges, and Australian PR status from day one.

3. Subclass 494 - Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (SESR)

Designed to assist businesses across regional Australia (outside Sydney, Melbourne, and Brisbane), the Subclass 494 visa allows employers to sponsor overseas professionals for a 5-year provisional visa. After three years of living and working in regional Australia, holders are eligible for permanent residency through the Subclass 191 visa.
