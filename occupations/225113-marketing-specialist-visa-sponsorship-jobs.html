Program Overview & Sponsorship Criteria

Migration Skills List Short-term Skilled Occupation List (STSOL) / Core Skills
Remuneration Benchmark TSMIT $73,150 AUD + Super (Market range: $85,000 - $130,000)

Australian Visa Sponsorship Pathways

Subclass 482 TSS to 186 TRT transition, Subclass 494 Regional SESR to 191 PR.

Under current Department of Home Affairs guidelines, qualified overseas candidates nominated for ANZSCO 225113 by an approved Standard Business Sponsor (SBS) may access:

  • Subclass 482 (TSS Visa): Employer-sponsored 2 to 4 year work authorization with full family secondary work and schooling rights.
  • Subclass 186 (ENS PR): Permanent residency transition via the Temporary Residence Transition (TRT) stream after 2 years or direct entry nomination.
  • Subclass 494 (Regional SESR): 5-year provisional regional visa leading directly to Subclass 191 Permanent Residency after 3 qualifying years.

Official Australian Workplace Scope & Duties

  • Formulating multi-channel marketing campaigns, brand positioning, and go-to-market strategies.
  • Analyzing customer analytics, conversion rates, and return on marketing investment (ROAS).
  • Managing paid media, organic SEO, content distribution, and partner channels.

Skills Assessment & Qualification Standards

Assessed by VETASSESS under Group B, requiring a Bachelor degree in Marketing, Business, or Communications.

📋 Candidate Compliance Tip:

Australian employers require verified proof of English proficiency (IELTS/PTE), genuine qualifications, and an official skills assessment outcome letter from VETASSESS to lodge Subclass 482/186 nominations.
