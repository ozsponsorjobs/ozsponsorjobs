Program Overview & Sponsorship Criteria

ANZSCO Unit Code 253399
Assessing Authority Medical Board of Australia (MBA) / AMC
Migration Skills List Priority Migration Skilled Occupation List (PMSOL)
Remuneration Benchmark $220,000 - $450,000+ AUD depending on specialty and state health award

Australian Visa Sponsorship Pathways

Subclass 186 ENS Direct Entry PR, Subclass 482 TSS, and Subclass 494 Regional.

Under current Department of Home Affairs guidelines, qualified overseas candidates nominated for ANZSCO 253399 by an approved Standard Business Sponsor (SBS) may access:

  • Subclass 482 (TSS Visa): Employer-sponsored 2 to 4 year work authorization with full family secondary work and schooling rights.
  • Subclass 186 (ENS PR): Permanent residency transition via the Temporary Residence Transition (TRT) stream after 2 years or direct entry nomination.
  • Subclass 494 (Regional SESR): 5-year provisional regional visa leading directly to Subclass 191 Permanent Residency after 3 qualifying years.

Official Australian Workplace Scope & Duties

  • Providing specialized medical diagnosis and patient treatment in clinical sub-specialties.
  • Conducting complex clinical rounds, emergency admissions, and diagnostic procedures.
  • Collaborating with tertiary medical faculties and mentoring junior registrars and interns.

Skills Assessment & Qualification Standards

Assessed through the relevant specialist medical college (e.g. RACP, RANZCP, RACS) via the Specialist Pathway for substantial equivalence.

📋 Candidate Compliance Tip:

Australian employers require verified proof of English proficiency (IELTS/PTE), genuine qualifications, and an official skills assessment outcome letter from Medical Board of Australia (MBA) / AMC to lodge Subclass 482/186 nominations.
