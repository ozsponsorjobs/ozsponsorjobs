Australian Visa Sponsorship Pathways

Fast-track Subclass 186 Direct Entry PR, Subclass 482 TSS, and Subclass 494 Regional with expedited government visa processing.

Under current Department of Home Affairs guidelines, qualified overseas candidates nominated for ANZSCO 253111 by an approved Standard Business Sponsor (SBS) may access:

  • Subclass 482 (TSS Visa): Employer-sponsored 2 to 4 year work authorization with full family secondary work and schooling rights.
  • Subclass 186 (ENS PR): Permanent residency transition via the Temporary Residence Transition (TRT) stream after 2 years or direct entry nomination.
  • Subclass 494 (Regional SESR): 5-year provisional regional visa leading directly to Subclass 191 Permanent Residency after 3 qualifying years.

Official Australian Workplace Scope & Duties

  • Diagnosing and treating physical and psychological illnesses, disorders, and acute injuries.
  • Prescribing medicinal treatments, ordering pathology and imaging investigations, and performing minor surgery.
  • Managing chronic diseases (diabetes, cardiovascular conditions, hypertension) and health prevention.
  • Referring patients to specialist physicians, allied healthcare providers, and acute hospital wards.

Skills Assessment & Qualification Standards

International Medical Graduates (IMGs) must gain registration with the Medical Board of Australia via the Competent Authority, Standard, or Specialist Pathways with RACGP/ACRRM fellowship.

📋 Candidate Compliance Tip:

Australian employers require verified proof of English proficiency (IELTS/PTE), genuine qualifications, and an official skills assessment outcome letter from Medical Board of Australia (MBA) / AMC to lodge Subclass 482/186 nominations.
