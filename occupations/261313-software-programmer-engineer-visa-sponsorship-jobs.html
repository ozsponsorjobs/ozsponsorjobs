ANZSCO 261313: Software & Applications Programmer Sponsorship Jobs

Apply for verified ANZSCO 261313 Software Engineer and Programmer visa sponsorship jobs in Sydney, Melbourne, and Brisbane. Subclass 482, 186 ENS, and ACS assessment guidance.

Program Overview & Sponsorship Criteria

ANZSCO Unit Code 261313
Assessing Authority Australian Computer Society (ACS)
Migration Skills List Medium and Long-term Strategic Skills List (MLTSSL)
Remuneration Benchmark TSMIT $73,150 AUD + Super (Market range: $110,000 - $175,000)

Australian Visa Sponsorship Pathways

Subclass 186 ENS Direct Entry PR, Subclass 482 TSS to 186 TRT after 2 years, Subclass 189/190 points stream, or Subclass 494 Regional.

Under current Department of Home Affairs guidelines, qualified overseas candidates nominated for ANZSCO 261313 by an approved Standard Business Sponsor (SBS) may access:

  • Subclass 482 (TSS Visa): Employer-sponsored 2 to 4 year work authorization with full family secondary work and schooling rights.
  • Subclass 186 (ENS PR): Permanent residency transition via the Temporary Residence Transition (TRT) stream after 2 years or direct entry nomination.
  • Subclass 494 (Regional SESR): 5-year provisional regional visa leading directly to Subclass 191 Permanent Residency after 3 qualifying years.

Official Australian Workplace Scope & Duties

  • Designing, architecting, and writing scalable backend and frontend software services.
  • Implementing cloud architectures (AWS, Azure, GCP), microservices, and CI/CD pipelines.
  • Collaborating with product managers and UX designers using agile frameworks.
  • Conducting automated testing, code reviews, and resolving system bottlenecks.

Skills Assessment & Qualification Standards

Australian Computer Society (ACS) evaluates tertiary ICT qualifications and post-qualification work experience via the ACS Migration Skills Assessment.

📋 Candidate Compliance Tip:

Australian employers require verified proof of English proficiency (IELTS/PTE), genuine qualifications, and an official skills assessment outcome letter from Australian Computer Society (ACS) to lodge Subclass 482/186 nominations.
