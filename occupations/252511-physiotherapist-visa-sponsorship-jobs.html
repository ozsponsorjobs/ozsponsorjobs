Australian Visa Sponsorship Pathways

Subclass 186 ENS Direct Entry PR, Subclass 482 TSS to 186 TRT, and Subclass 494 Regional PR.

Under current Department of Home Affairs guidelines, qualified overseas candidates nominated for ANZSCO 252511 by an approved Standard Business Sponsor (SBS) may access:

  • Subclass 482 (TSS Visa): Employer-sponsored 2 to 4 year work authorization with full family secondary work and schooling rights.
  • Subclass 186 (ENS PR): Permanent residency transition via the Temporary Residence Transition (TRT) stream after 2 years or direct entry nomination.
  • Subclass 494 (Regional SESR): 5-year provisional regional visa leading directly to Subclass 191 Permanent Residency after 3 qualifying years.

Official Australian Workplace Scope & Duties

  • Assessing physical dysfunction, musculoskeletal injuries, and neuromuscular disorders.
  • Designing tailored exercise rehabilitation programs and manual therapy treatments.
  • Educating patients on injury prevention, ergonomics, and post-surgical recovery.

Skills Assessment & Qualification Standards

Australian Physiotherapy Council (APC) conducts skills assessments requiring verification of university qualifications and completion of the APC Clinical & Written Examinations.

📋 Candidate Compliance Tip:

Australian employers require verified proof of English proficiency (IELTS/PTE), genuine qualifications, and an official skills assessment outcome letter from Australian Physiotherapy Council (APC) to lodge Subclass 482/186 nominations.
