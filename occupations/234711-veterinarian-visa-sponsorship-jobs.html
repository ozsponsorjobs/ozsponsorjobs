Australian Visa Sponsorship Pathways

Subclass 186 ENS Direct Entry PR, Subclass 482 TSS, and Subclass 494 Regional.

Under current Department of Home Affairs guidelines, qualified overseas candidates nominated for ANZSCO 234711 by an approved Standard Business Sponsor (SBS) may access:

  • Subclass 482 (TSS Visa): Employer-sponsored 2 to 4 year work authorization with full family secondary work and schooling rights.
  • Subclass 186 (ENS PR): Permanent residency transition via the Temporary Residence Transition (TRT) stream after 2 years or direct entry nomination.
  • Subclass 494 (Regional SESR): 5-year provisional regional visa leading directly to Subclass 191 Permanent Residency after 3 qualifying years.

Official Australian Workplace Scope & Duties

  • Diagnosing and medically treating companion animals, equine, or livestock.
  • Performing soft-tissue and orthopedic surgeries, anesthesiology, and wound repair.
  • Advising pet owners on vaccination schedules, animal welfare, and biosafety.

Skills Assessment & Qualification Standards

Assessed by the Australasian Veterinary Boards Council (AVBC). Degrees accredited by AVMA, RCVS, or EAEVE generally enjoy direct registration recognition.

📋 Candidate Compliance Tip:

Australian employers require verified proof of English proficiency (IELTS/PTE), genuine qualifications, and an official skills assessment outcome letter from Australasian Veterinary Boards Council (AVBC) to lodge Subclass 482/186 nominations.
