Australian Visa Sponsorship Pathways

Subclass 482 TSS to 186 TRT transition and Subclass 494 Regional.

Under current Department of Home Affairs guidelines, qualified overseas candidates nominated for ANZSCO 225412 by an approved Standard Business Sponsor (SBS) may access:

  • Subclass 482 (TSS Visa): Employer-sponsored 2 to 4 year work authorization with full family secondary work and schooling rights.
  • Subclass 186 (ENS PR): Permanent residency transition via the Temporary Residence Transition (TRT) stream after 2 years or direct entry nomination.
  • Subclass 494 (Regional SESR): 5-year provisional regional visa leading directly to Subclass 191 Permanent Residency after 3 qualifying years.

Official Australian Workplace Scope & Duties

  • Representing industrial equipment, chemical, and building supply manufacturers.
  • Interpreting customer technical requirements and preparing engineered product bids.
  • Managing wholesale client territories and after-sales service agreements.

Skills Assessment & Qualification Standards

Assessed by VETASSESS under Group B.

📋 Candidate Compliance Tip:

Australian employers require verified proof of English proficiency (IELTS/PTE), genuine qualifications, and an official skills assessment outcome letter from VETASSESS to lodge Subclass 482/186 nominations.
