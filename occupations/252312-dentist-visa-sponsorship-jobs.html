ANZSCO 252312: Dentist Visa Sponsorship Jobs in Australia

Apply for ANZSCO 252312 Dentist visa sponsorship jobs across private dental networks and regional clinics under Subclass 482, 186, and 494.

Program Overview & Sponsorship Criteria

ANZSCO Unit Code 252312
Assessing Authority Australian Dental Council (ADC)
Migration Skills List Medium and Long-term Strategic Skills List (MLTSSL)
Remuneration Benchmark $120,000 - $250,000+ AUD (Commission or base salary)

Australian Visa Sponsorship Pathways

Subclass 186 ENS Direct Entry PR, Subclass 482 TSS, and Subclass 494 Regional.

Under current Department of Home Affairs guidelines, qualified overseas candidates nominated for ANZSCO 252312 by an approved Standard Business Sponsor (SBS) may access:

  • Subclass 482 (TSS Visa): Employer-sponsored 2 to 4 year work authorization with full family secondary work and schooling rights.
  • Subclass 186 (ENS PR): Permanent residency transition via the Temporary Residence Transition (TRT) stream after 2 years or direct entry nomination.
  • Subclass 494 (Regional SESR): 5-year provisional regional visa leading directly to Subclass 191 Permanent Residency after 3 qualifying years.

Official Australian Workplace Scope & Duties

  • Diagnosing dental diseases, restorative treatments, endodontics, and prosthodontics.
  • Performing dental extractions, oral surgery, and preventive hygiene therapies.
  • Interpreting dental radiographs and coordinating orthodontic and periodontal referrals.

Skills Assessment & Qualification Standards

Requires completion of the Australian Dental Council (ADC) examination process or qualification recognized by the Dental Board of Australia (DBA).

📋 Candidate Compliance Tip:

Australian employers require verified proof of English proficiency (IELTS/PTE), genuine qualifications, and an official skills assessment outcome letter from Australian Dental Council (ADC) to lodge Subclass 482/186 nominations.
