ANZSCO 331211: Carpenter & Joiner Visa Sponsorship Jobs Australia

Find residential and commercial builders sponsoring ANZSCO 331211 Carpenters and Joiners under Subclass 482, 186 Direct Entry PR, and 494.

Program Overview & Sponsorship Criteria

ANZSCO Unit Code 331211
Assessing Authority Trades Recognition Australia (TRA)
Migration Skills List Medium and Long-term Strategic Skills List (MLTSSL)
Remuneration Benchmark TSMIT $73,150 AUD + Super (Market range: $78,000 - $120,000)

Australian Visa Sponsorship Pathways

Subclass 186 ENS Direct Entry PR, Subclass 482 TSS, and Subclass 494 Regional.

Under current Department of Home Affairs guidelines, qualified overseas candidates nominated for ANZSCO 331211 by an approved Standard Business Sponsor (SBS) may access:

  • Subclass 482 (TSS Visa): Employer-sponsored 2 to 4 year work authorization with full family secondary work and schooling rights.
  • Subclass 186 (ENS PR): Permanent residency transition via the Temporary Residence Transition (TRT) stream after 2 years or direct entry nomination.
  • Subclass 494 (Regional SESR): 5-year provisional regional visa leading directly to Subclass 191 Permanent Residency after 3 qualifying years.

Official Australian Workplace Scope & Duties

  • Constructing structural timber frameworks, wall frames, roof trusses, and subfloors.
  • Installing doors, windows, architectural cladding, architraves, and interior cabinetry.
  • Operating laser leveling devices, table saws, nail guns, and hand tools.

Skills Assessment & Qualification Standards

Assessed by TRA via Certificate III in Carpentry or international carpentry apprenticeship.

📋 Candidate Compliance Tip:

Australian employers require verified proof of English proficiency (IELTS/PTE), genuine qualifications, and an official skills assessment outcome letter from Trades Recognition Australia (TRA) to lodge Subclass 482/186 nominations.
