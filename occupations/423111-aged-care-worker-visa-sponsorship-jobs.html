ANZSCO 423111: Aged Care & Disability Support Visa Sponsorship Jobs

Apply for ANZSCO 423111 Aged Care and Disability Support Worker visa sponsorship under the Aged Care Industry Labour Agreement with direct PR pathways in Australia.

Program Overview & Sponsorship Criteria

ANZSCO Unit Code 423111
Assessing Authority ANMAC / ACWA (Labour Agreement)
Migration Skills List Aged Care Industry Labour Agreement (Direct PR Pathway)
Remuneration Benchmark TSMIT $73,150 AUD or Labour Agreement conceeded award rate + penalties

Australian Visa Sponsorship Pathways

Aged Care Industry Labour Agreement: Direct transition to Subclass 186 PR after 2 years of qualifying sponsorship with an approved aged care provider.

Under current Department of Home Affairs guidelines, qualified overseas candidates nominated for ANZSCO 423111 by an approved Standard Business Sponsor (SBS) may access:

  • Subclass 482 (TSS Visa): Employer-sponsored 2 to 4 year work authorization with full family secondary work and schooling rights.
  • Subclass 186 (ENS PR): Permanent residency transition via the Temporary Residence Transition (TRT) stream after 2 years or direct entry nomination.
  • Subclass 494 (Regional SESR): 5-year provisional regional visa leading directly to Subclass 191 Permanent Residency after 3 qualifying years.

Official Australian Workplace Scope & Duties

  • Assisting elderly and disabled residents with personal hygiene, mobility, and daily living activities.
  • Supporting medication administration under clinical registered nurse supervision.
  • Facilitating emotional well-being, social companionship, and recreational therapy.
  • Documenting resident health updates in electronic aged care management systems.

Skills Assessment & Qualification Standards

Employers access the dedicated Aged Care Industry Labour Agreement offering English language and salary concessions, requiring an Australian Certificate III in Individual Support (Ageing/Disability) or comparable qualification.

📋 Candidate Compliance Tip:

Australian employers require verified proof of English proficiency (IELTS/PTE), genuine qualifications, and an official skills assessment outcome letter from ANMAC / ACWA (Labour Agreement) to lodge Subclass 482/186 nominations.
