Explore Australian NDIS providers, clinics, and health departments sponsoring ANZSCO 252411 Occupational Therapists under Subclass 482, 186, and 494.

Program Overview & Sponsorship Criteria

ANZSCO Unit Code 252411
Assessing Authority Occupational Therapy Council (OTC)
Migration Skills List Medium and Long-term Strategic Skills List (MLTSSL)
Remuneration Benchmark TSMIT $73,150 AUD + Super (Market range: $82,000 - $115,000)

Australian Visa Sponsorship Pathways

Subclass 186 ENS Direct Entry PR, Subclass 482 TSS, and Subclass 494 Regional SESR.

Under current Department of Home Affairs guidelines, qualified overseas candidates nominated for ANZSCO 252411 by an approved Standard Business Sponsor (SBS) may access:

  • Subclass 482 (TSS Visa): Employer-sponsored 2 to 4 year work authorization with full family secondary work and schooling rights.
  • Subclass 186 (ENS PR): Permanent residency transition via the Temporary Residence Transition (TRT) stream after 2 years or direct entry nomination.
  • Subclass 494 (Regional SESR): 5-year provisional regional visa leading directly to Subclass 191 Permanent Residency after 3 qualifying years.

Official Australian Workplace Scope & Duties

  • Evaluating physical, cognitive, and sensory function in clients across all ages.
  • Developing compensatory strategies, assistive technology solutions, and home modifications.
  • Delivering NDIS functional capacity assessments and goal-oriented therapy plans.

Skills Assessment & Qualification Standards

Assessed by the Occupational Therapy Council (OTC) through Stage 1 Desktop Assessment and Stage 2 Supervised Practice period for overseas-trained OTs.

📋 Candidate Compliance Tip:

Australian employers require verified proof of English proficiency (IELTS/PTE), genuine qualifications, and an official skills assessment outcome letter from Occupational Therapy Council (OTC) to lodge Subclass 482/186 nominations.
