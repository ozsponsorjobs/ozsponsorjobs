Australian Visa Sponsorship Pathways

Subclass 186 ENS Direct Entry PR, Subclass 482 TSS, and Subclass 494 Regional.

Under current Department of Home Affairs guidelines, qualified overseas candidates nominated for ANZSCO 272511 by an approved Standard Business Sponsor (SBS) may access:

  • Subclass 482 (TSS Visa): Employer-sponsored 2 to 4 year work authorization with full family secondary work and schooling rights.
  • Subclass 186 (ENS PR): Permanent residency transition via the Temporary Residence Transition (TRT) stream after 2 years or direct entry nomination.
  • Subclass 494 (Regional SESR): 5-year provisional regional visa leading directly to Subclass 191 Permanent Residency after 3 qualifying years.

Official Australian Workplace Scope & Duties

  • Conducting risk assessments and safety plans for vulnerable children and families.
  • Navigating statutory child protection legislation and court proceedings.
  • Connecting families with mental health, housing, and domestic violence support.

Skills Assessment & Qualification Standards

Australian Association of Social Workers (AASW) requires an accredited Bachelor or Master of Social Work including minimum 980 hours of supervised field placement.

📋 Candidate Compliance Tip:

Australian employers require verified proof of English proficiency (IELTS/PTE), genuine qualifications, and an official skills assessment outcome letter from Australian Association of Social Workers (AASW) to lodge Subclass 482/186 nominations.
