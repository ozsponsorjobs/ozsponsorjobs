ANZSCO 254415: Registered Nurse Visa Sponsorship & Fast-Track PR

Explore high-priority Australian hospital networks and healthcare providers sponsoring ANZSCO 254415 Registered Nurses under Subclass 482, 186 ENS, and 494.

Program Overview & Sponsorship Criteria

ANZSCO Unit Code 254415
Assessing Authority Australian Nursing and Midwifery Accreditation Council (ANMAC)
Migration Skills List Priority Migration Skilled Occupation List (PMSOL) / MLTSSL
Remuneration Benchmark TSMIT $73,150 AUD + Super (Market range: $85,000 - $125,000 + penalties)

Australian Visa Sponsorship Pathways

Immediate Direct Entry PR via Subclass 186 ENS, Subclass 482 TSS with fast-track processing, Subclass 189/190 points stream, or Subclass 494 regional.

Under current Department of Home Affairs guidelines, qualified overseas candidates nominated for ANZSCO 254415 by an approved Standard Business Sponsor (SBS) may access:

  • Subclass 482 (TSS Visa): Employer-sponsored 2 to 4 year work authorization with full family secondary work and schooling rights.
  • Subclass 186 (ENS PR): Permanent residency transition via the Temporary Residence Transition (TRT) stream after 2 years or direct entry nomination.
  • Subclass 494 (Regional SESR): 5-year provisional regional visa leading directly to Subclass 191 Permanent Residency after 3 qualifying years.

Official Australian Workplace Scope & Duties

  • Assessing, planning, implementing, and evaluating nursing care for acute and chronic patients.
  • Administering intravenous medications, monitoring clinical diagnostics, and assisting medical officers.
  • Leading multidisciplinary clinical handovers and maintaining accurate electronic medical records (EMR).
  • Supervising enrolled nurses, healthcare assistants, and nursing students across clinical wards.

Skills Assessment & Qualification Standards

ANMAC skills assessment requires AHPRA registration eligibility. Overseas nurses typically qualify through the Outcome-Based Assessment (OBA) pathway comprising NCLEX-RN and OSCE examinations.

📋 Candidate Compliance Tip:

Australian employers require verified proof of English proficiency (IELTS/PTE), genuine qualifications, and an official skills assessment outcome letter from Australian Nursing and Midwifery Accreditation Council (ANMAC) to lodge Subclass 482/186 nominations.
