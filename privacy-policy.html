Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 • Compliant with GDPR, Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), and Google AdSense

At OZ Sponsor Jobs, accessible from https://www.ozsponsorjobs.site/, one of our top priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by OZ Sponsor Jobs and how we use, safeguard, and disclose it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@ozsponsorjobs.site.

1. Scope & Applicable Regulations

This policy applies to all visitors, job seekers, and employers interacting with our platform globally. We comply with:

  • Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) contained in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU Regulation 2016/679) for visitors within the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK.
  • Google AdSense Publisher Policies regarding advertising cookies and user consent.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal and non-personal data in the following circumstances:

  • Information You Provide Voluntarily: When you submit a contact form, job inquiry, or employer listing, you may provide your name, email address, company name, phone number, and message contents.
  • Log Files: Like standard web platforms, OZ Sponsor Jobs uses log files. The information inside log files includes Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and click counts. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information.
  • Cookies & Local Storage: We use cookies and browser local storage to remember your job search filters, bookmarks, and display preferences.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Vendors

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, commonly known as the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve ads to our site visitors based on their visit to www.ozsponsorjobs.site and other sites on the internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

OZ Sponsor Jobs has no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers. You should consult the respective privacy policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information.

4. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected information to:

  • Operate, maintain, and enhance the performance of OZ Sponsor Jobs.
  • Understand and analyze how visitors interact with our search and filter tools.
  • Respond directly to candidate inquiries, employer partnership proposals, and support tickets.
  • Prevent fraudulent job listings and enhance platform cybersecurity.
  • Comply with applicable legal, fiscal, and regulatory obligations in Australia.

5. Your GDPR Privacy Rights (EEA & UK Users)

If you reside within the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you are entitled to the following data protection rights:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data held by us.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • The right to erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict or object to processing: You have the right to request that we restrict or cease the processing of your personal data.
  • The right to data portability: You have the right to request the transfer of collected data to another organization or directly to you.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at support@ozsponsorjobs.site. We respond to verified requests within 30 days.

6. Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) Compliance

In accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), we ensure personal information is handled transparently. We will not sell, rent, or trade your personal details to third parties for commercial marketing purposes without your prior explicit consent.

7. Children's Privacy

Protecting children online is paramount. OZ Sponsor Jobs is directed at adults seeking professional employment and skilled migration pathways. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 16. If you believe your child has provided personal details on our site, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such records.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological, legal, or operational modifications. Any changes will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.
